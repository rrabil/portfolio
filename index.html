Process

How I approach a documentation problem

  1. 01

    Find the reader's actual job

    Clarify who needs the content, what they are trying to complete, and what can go wrong.

  2. 02

    Shape the information model

    Separate conceptual context, procedural steps, and reference facts so each piece can do its job.

  3. 03

    Draft for verification

    Make assumptions visible, invite precise review, and keep the content easy to test against the product.

  4. 04

    Publish with maintenance in mind

    Leave a structure that future updates can follow without turning into a scavenger hunt.
