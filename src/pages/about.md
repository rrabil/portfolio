## Why I Write

I'm a writer at heart, and always will be–a technical writer who appreciates the role of science and technology in society, and wants to help people [use it correctly](https://everypageispageone.com/2014/02/10/the-goal-of-tech-comm-is-to-enable-correct-action/) (one scholar calls this the process of "[accommodating technology to the user](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/345423633_What's_Technical_About_Technical_Writing)"). [I've long believed](https://richardrabil.com/2017/10/20/get-into-technical-writing/) that a culture like ours needs talented, conscientious communicators who can write clearly and effectively about the things we build. That's one reason why, after finishing my BA in professional writing, I went on to pursue a masters degree in technical communication.

## A Systems Mindset

I have also always been interested in how to do documentation and knowledge management in a sustainable way over the long term. For as long as I've been a technical writer, I've been asking systems questions such as:

* How do we scale this ecosystem of content after publishing the first version? How should it be organized to grow and expand as easily as possible over time? What if we need to migrate to a different repository or format?
* What does the next writer or editor who needs to maintain this documentation in the future need to know? What style guidance should they use? What templates and content rules should they follow? What experts should they rely on for review?
* How do we track the health of the enormous volumes of information under our responsibility? When and how do we need retire information when it is no longer necessary?

This mindset has led me into many overlapping disciplines, from technical editing and information architecture to content strategy and knowledge management. I have frequently taken on "team lead" roles for large-scale documentation projects in the organizations I work for: setting up governance frameworks, defining the content strategy for new efforts and team collaborators (consisting of full-time writers as well as non-writer contributors), and sharing best practices and articles about the latest trends in the field.

## Where AI Fits In

The explosive growth of AI has taken my systems mindset to a new level. In addition to researching and publishing user-friendly docs, I now regularly apply my systems thinking and editorial judgment to develop prompts, skills, agents, and workflows to help entire teams generate and publish content consistently and at scale. I am constantly asking: Where can we use the strengths of AI to elevate documentation and serve end users better, in a way that is sustainable for us to support? And how we can be sure that the "fuel" which powers AI–the underlying knowledge and documentation–is as healthy as it can be so that users trust it?

This combination–seasoned technical writer and AI-augmented knowledge architect–is what I can bring to your organization. And in this portfolio I have [focused on](/how-i-built-this) putting the principles of quality and sustainability into practice.
